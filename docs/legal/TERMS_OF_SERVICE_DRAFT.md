# Terms of service — draft for legal review

Status: DRAFT, 2026-10-29. Replaces the March 2025 text, which says
nothing about money (P17-13). Two audiences, two documents in the end:
customers and businesses. Brackets mark decisions and legal questions.

## A. Customers

1. **The service.** Maw3ed lets you find businesses and book
   appointments with them. The appointment is a contract between you
   and the business; Maw3ed is the platform [LEGAL: intermediary status
   and liability under Egyptian law].
2. **Your account.** You sign in with a one-time code sent to your
   phone. You are responsible for the phone number; tell us if you lose
   control of it. We may end sessions and accounts that abuse the
   service.
3. **Bookings.** A booking is confirmed when the page or app says
   "confirmed" — not before. Some businesses confirm by hand; some ask
   for a deposit first (below). You can cancel or move a booking from
   its link until **two hours** before it starts [FACT: `CUTOFF_HOURS = 2`;
   DECISION: keep or make per business]. Businesses may cancel too; you
   are told at once.
4. **Deposits and payments.** Some businesses ask for a deposit (a
   percentage set by the business, shown before you pay). Payment is
   taken by Paymob; we never see your card. If your payment fails or you
   do not complete it within about an hour, the slot is released. **A
   deposit is not refunded automatically when you cancel** [FACT:
   cancellation flags the payment for a decision; no automatic refund];
   whether it is refunded is the business's policy [DECISION: platform
   minimum, e.g. full refund if cancelled ≥ 24 h ahead; LEGAL: Consumer
   Protection Law requirements]. Refunds go back by the method you paid
   with, through Paymob, within [N] business days once decided.
5. **No-shows.** If you do not come, the business may keep the deposit
   [DECISION] and may decline future bookings.
6. **Reviews.** Only after a completed booking; honest, your own
   experience, no personal data about others; we may remove reviews that
   break these rules and businesses may reply publicly.
7. **Your data.** The privacy policy applies.
8. **Liability.** [LEGAL: the usual limitations, to the extent the law
   allows, and the carve-outs it does not.]
9. **Law and disputes.** [LEGAL: Egyptian law; venue; consumer dispute
   routes.]

## B. Businesses

1. **Listing.** You confirm you are entitled to represent the business
   and that what you publish (names, prices, photos, hours) is accurate.
   We may unpublish a listing that is not.
2. **Plans and billing.** Free, Pro and Premium as shown on `/pricing`;
   paid monthly or yearly (ten months' price for twelve) through Paymob;
   the plan runs until the period end and does not renew automatically
   [FACT: renewal is a new payment]; the free plan takes up to 30 online
   bookings a month, after which bookings wait for your confirmation
   [FACT: soft cap]. Refunds of subscription fees: [DECISION].
3. **Customer data.** The customer records in your dashboard are yours
   to use for the booking relationship and for no other purpose without
   the customer's consent [LEGAL: your obligations as controller under
   Law 151/2020; ours as processor]. Staff you give access to see what
   their role allows; you are responsible for them.
4. **Deposits you take.** You set the percentage and your refund policy;
   you must state it to customers [DECISION: platform minimum]. Money
   is settled to you by Paymob under your Paymob agreement [LEGAL: who
   is the merchant of record — today the platform's Paymob account].
5. **Cancellations by you.** Tell the customer in good time; repeated
   late cancellations may affect your listing.
6. **Acceptable use.** No fake bookings, reviews or listings; no
   scraping; no messaging customers through our channels for marketing
   [DECISION].
7. **Termination.** Either side may end the subscription at the period
   end; we may suspend a listing for breach, with notice where we can.
8. **Liability, law, disputes.** [LEGAL.]
